Frequently Asked Questions

Does VoiCat Private need an internet connection?

No. Transcription, translation, and summarization all run on your device using Apple's own frameworks. An internet connection is only used to show ads and send optional, anonymous app analytics — never to process your audio or text.

Is my audio or transcript ever sent anywhere?

No. VoiCat Private has no cloud AI backend. Your recordings, transcripts, translations, and summaries are processed and stored entirely on your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

Why does it say summarization isn't supported on my device?

Summaries are powered by Apple Intelligence, which requires a compatible iPhone model and iOS version. On devices without Apple Intelligence, summarization is simply unavailable — transcription and translation are unaffected and continue to work normally.

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Remove Ads inside the app. It's a single one-time purchase — not a subscription — processed securely through Apple's App Store.

I want a refund for my purchase. How do I get one?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select VoiCat Private to request a refund.

How do I import from WhatsApp?

In WhatsApp, open a voice message → tap the three dots → Share → VoiCat Private. You can also use the Import button in the app and choose "Files" to select any audio file from your device.

Why is my transcription inaccurate?

Accuracy depends on audio clarity, background noise, and language, since transcription runs through Apple's on-device Speech recognizer. For best results, record in a quiet environment and speak clearly.

What's the difference between VoiCat Private and VoiCat?

VoiCat Private runs everything on-device with no subscription — free with ads, or one-time purchase to remove them. VoiCat uses cloud AI and offers subscription tiers with larger monthly quotas. See the comparison on our home page.
