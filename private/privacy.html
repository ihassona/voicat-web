Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Overview

VoiCat Private ("we", "our", or "us") is a separate app from VoiCat. This policy covers VoiCat Private only. VoiCat Private is built around a simple rule: your audio and the text produced from it never leave your device.

VoiCat Private does not require account registration and has no server backend for AI processing. Transcription, translation, and summarization all run on-device using Apple's own frameworks.

Data We Collect

We process only the data necessary to run the app and keep it funded:

  • Your voice notes — recordings, transcripts, translations, summaries, and edits are stored only in the app on your device. We never receive a copy of this content, and it is never transmitted to us or to any AI provider.
  • Anonymous app analytics — we use Google Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage (e.g. which screens are opened, how often features are used) in aggregate. This does not include your voice notes, transcripts, or any note content.
  • Advertising data — VoiCat Private shows ads through Google AdMob to remain free. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, subject to the permission you grant through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline tracking, you still see ads, but they are not personalized to you.
  • Purchase data — if you buy "Remove Ads," Apple processes the payment through StoreKit. We receive only a transaction confirmation to unlock the purchase on your device; we never see your payment card details or Apple Account email address.
  • Push notification token (optional) — if you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a token so we can occasionally send product updates. No note content is ever included in a notification payload.

We do not ask you to create a VoiCat Private account, and we do not collect your name, location, or payment details for our own use.

How On-Device AI Works

VoiCat Private has no cloud AI backend. Every AI feature runs locally on your iPhone using Apple's system frameworks:

  • Transcription — uses Apple's Speech framework (on-device speech recognition). Your audio is processed by iOS itself and never uploaded anywhere.
  • Translation — uses Apple's Translation framework. Language models may be downloaded by iOS directly from Apple to your device the first time you use a language pair; translation itself still happens on-device.
  • Summarization — uses Apple Intelligence (on-device). This feature is only available on Apple Intelligence–compatible devices and iOS versions; on unsupported devices, summarization is simply unavailable, and nothing is sent to a server as a fallback.
Because processing happens entirely on-device, we have no visibility into your recordings, transcripts, translations, or summaries at any point.

Local Storage

Voice notes, transcriptions, translations, summaries, and edits are stored in VoiCat Private on your device using Apple's local storage APIs. This data:

  • Is included in your iCloud backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled.
  • Is deleted from the app when you delete a note; removing the app also removes its local app data, subject to any device or iCloud backups you control.
  • Is never synced to, or retained by, any server we operate — we don't operate one for your note content.

Ads & In-App Purchase

VoiCat Private is free to use and supported by interstitial ads served through Google AdMob. You can remove ads permanently with a single one-time, non-renewing in-app purchase processed by Apple's App Store. There is no subscription and no recurring billing for VoiCat Private.

Third-Party Services

We require these providers to handle shared data under privacy and security protections that are the same as or equivalent to the protections described in this policy. They may use data only to provide, secure, and support the requested service, subject to their applicable terms and legal obligations.

VoiCat Private uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — ad serving and measurement
  • Google Firebase Analytics — anonymous, aggregate app usage analytics
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — optional push notifications
  • Apple StoreKit — in-app purchase processing

None of these providers receive your voice recordings, transcripts, translations, or summaries — that content never leaves your device.

Your Rights

You can delete individual notes or all local notes at any time from the app's Settings. You may also disable notifications and reset Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission at any time in iOS Settings. We will respond to applicable privacy requests, subject to legal, security, and fraud-prevention requirements.

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us.

Children's Privacy

VoiCat Private is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used our app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of VoiCat Private after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
